Before you book

Villa policies & guest responsibility

Please read these important rules before confirming your reservation. The wording below summarizes the policy sheet provided by the property.

💳 Booking & payment
  • 50% down payment is required to confirm a booking.
  • Bookings are valid for 2 days pending down payment; unpaid bookings are automatically cancelled.
  • Down payment is strictly non-refundable.
  • One rebooking is allowed at no cost; a second rebooking costs ₱1,000.
  • Settle the remaining balance at least 3 days before check-in or upon check-in, as stated in the supplied policy; confirm the exact deadline with management.
🕒 Check-in & pool rules
  • Overnight: check-in 2:00 PM; check-out 11:00 AM.
  • Day use: check-in 9:00 AM; check-out 5:00 PM.
  • Pool falls are turned off at 10:00 PM, but guests may continue using the pool overnight according to the sheet.
  • Pets are not allowed to swim in the pool.
  • Children must not be left unsupervised in the pool, playground, or garden areas.
🍽️ Food, drinks & noise
  • No corkage for lechon, food trays, and non-alcoholic drinks.
  • Outside catering is charged ₱1,000, except dessert carts.
  • Only portable speakers are allowed; videoke machines are not allowed. The sheet lists an ₱800 charge for videoke.
  • No loud music after 10:00 PM.
  • The sheet lists a ₱1,000 charge for alcoholic beverages, regardless of the number of bottles. Confirm how this rule is applied with management.
  • Do not feed stray dogs.
🛠️ Care for the property
  • Report damaged or broken items to management and arrange replacement or repair.
  • Follow staff instructions and all house rules.
  • Guests may be asked to leave without refund for illegal activity, dangerous behavior, violence, excessive disturbance, property damage, or serious misconduct, subject to applicable law.
🛡️ Safety & liability

The supplied policy says pool and facility use is at the guest’s own risk and parents/guardians are responsible for supervising minors. Guests are responsible for their conduct and damage during their stay. Management’s liability and any waiver remain subject to applicable law; this website summary is not a substitute for the full signed agreement.

👨‍👩‍👧 Minors & lawful use

Minors must be accompanied by a responsible parent/guardian and proper consent where required. Illegal activity, unsafe conduct, and activities that endanger other guests, staff, or the property are prohibited.

Guest acknowledgement: The supplied form asks the booking guest to confirm that they have read and understood the villa policy, liability waiver, and guest responsibilities, then provide a signature over printed name and booking date. Please request and complete the property’s official form with management.
